# Local typefaces

Downloaded from the Google Fonts source repository on 2026-10-26. Font binaries are unmodified; the accompanying OFL files apply to their respective families.

- Bodoni Moda variable, by Owen Earl: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bodonimoda
- Tinos regular, italic and bold, by Steve Matteson: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/tinos
- Tinos license: https://github.com/googlefonts/tinos/blob/main/OFL.txt
- Libre Bodoni variable, by Pablo Impallari and Rodrigo Fuenzalida: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/librebodoni (retained as the comparison candidate; not loaded by the production CSS).

The display face uses Bodoni Moda's original `opsz` and `wght` variable axes. Condensation is applied to real HTML text by the page layout. Tinos supplies the serif prose, navigation, buttons and italic quotation.
